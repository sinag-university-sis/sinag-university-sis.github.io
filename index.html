    Data Privacy Policy

    The Sinag University Student Information Management System processes student information to manage records, monitor enrollment, and display library clearance.

    Student records include the student number, name, course, year level, contact number, and enrollment status. Access to student records is restricted to authorized SIMS users.

    The Library System uses the student number to verify a student and sends clearance decisions to SIMS.

    For questions about your information or to request a correction, contact the school office responsible for student records.
